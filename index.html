Compliance Champions Cup

DWG-101 raffle, tournament style. Each Dawg brings the dog from their certificate and the aingegg their /blink summoned. The top two final scores were seeded at opposite ends of the bracket and everyone else was drawn at random. Going in you only see their blink distance. Dog touches, phishing and the quiz bonus get applied mid-battle, and the higher final score advances. The two finalists win.

Score = (blink px − dog touches + phishing) × (1 + quiz)

Phishing: +1 reported, 0 ignored, −1 clicked the link. Quiz is the first-try percent, 0 if unfinished. Summon grade by blink: E under 130 px, D 130+, C 215+, B 325+, A 435+, S 545+. Bracket draw seed 20261104.
